# lint-trap

Household laundry notes and fabric care references. Nothing exciting.

## Wash cycles

| Fabric | Temp | Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Cotton | Warm | Normal |
| Delicates | Cold | Gentle |
| Denim | Cold | Normal, inside out |
| Wool | Cold | Hand wash |

## Lint trap

Clean the dryer lint trap after every load. A full trap is a fire hazard and
dries clothes slower. Once a month, wash the trap screen with soap and water
to clear dryer-sheet residue.

## Stain guide

- Grease: dish soap, warm water, blot — never rub.
- Coffee: cold water rinse, then white vinegar.
- Ink: rubbing alcohol on a cotton ball, dab from the outside in.

## Notes

Fabric softener sheets leave residue on the lint screen. Vinegar in the rinse
cycle does the same job without the buildup.
