# lint-trap

Household notes on dryer lint trap maintenance. Started keeping this after the
dryer needed two full cycles to dry a load of towels — turned out the trap
screen had a film on it you couldn't see until you ran water over it.

## The routine

- Empty the lint trap before every load. Every load, no exceptions.
- Once a month: scrub the trap screen with warm water, dish soap, and a soft
  brush, then let it air dry. Dryer sheets leave an invisible residue that
  chokes airflow even when the screen looks clean.
- Twice a year (spring and fall): check the outside vent. If the flapper
  barely moves while the dryer is running, the duct needs cleaning.

## What I've noticed

- Average drying time dropped about 15 minutes per load after the first deep
  scrub of the screen.
- Towels and jeans are the canary: when they start coming out damp, the trap
  screen needs the soap treatment, not just a finger swipe.
- The "clean the trap" habit sticks better if the trash can is right next to
  the dryer. Moved ours in March, haven't missed a load since.
